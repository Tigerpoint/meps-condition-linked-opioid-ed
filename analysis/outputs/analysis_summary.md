# Survey-weighted analysis summary

## Cohort and design

The analytic cohort contained 4,454 complete cases from 4,468 eligible adults (0.31% excluded for main-covariate missingness).
The full pooled survey design had 117 strata, 400 stratum-PSU units, and 283 design degrees of freedom.

## Primary adjusted association

The standardized adjusted mean count ratio was 0.975 (95% CI 0.784 to 1.211), and the adjusted mean difference was -0.009 visits (95% CI -0.082 to 0.065).
The adjusted interval included no association on the ratio scale.

## Poverty interaction and any-ED outcome

The prespecified exposure-by-poverty interaction had p=0.5943. The direct exposure-by-poverty interaction did not provide clear evidence of different associations.
For any year-2 ED use, the standardized adjusted risk difference was -0.019 (95% CI -0.057 to 0.018).

## Interpretation boundary

These are associational estimates for observed condition-linked opioid prescriptions and subsequent annual all-cause ED use. They do not measure postoperative timing, opioid consumption, opioid-sparing care, or a causal effect.
